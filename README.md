# APEX HORIZON

Forza-style 3D circuit racer for phones. Original festival setting — chase cam, grip/drift physics, AI grid, garage, upgrades, and two road courses. Plays in the browser (landscape). No install.

## Play

1. Make this repo **public** (GitHub Pages on a private repo needs a paid plan).
2. Settings → Pages → Source: **GitHub Actions** (the workflow in `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys on every push to `main`).
3. Open https://wolfplayz07.github.io/Mobile-racing-game/
4. Add to Home Screen for a full-screen icon. Rotate to landscape.

Until Pages is on, open `index.html` from a static host. Three.js loads from a CDN, so the phone needs a network the first time.

## Drive

- **Gas** — hold the right pedal, or turn on Auto
- **Brake** — button above the pedal
- **Steer** — drag the left zone, or enable tilt (iPhone will ask for motion access)
- **Handbrake** — HB, for tight entries
- Keyboard also works: arrows / WASD, Space handbrake, Shift boost is not used

Race 3 laps. Credits bank in this browser and buy engine, tire, and aero upgrades in the garage.

## Tracks

- **Festival Ring** — technical park circuit, ~2.1 km
- **Red Canyon** — elevation and faster sweepers, ~2.6 km

## Cars

Horizon GT, Pulse EV, Vesper Muscle, Ridge Rally. Stats shift with upgrades.
